# Frozen descriptive IBL feedback specification v0.1

**Frozen:** 2026-07-26 before numerical contrast execution, but after inspecting
the selected sessions, outcome availability, and trial counts. This is therefore
not a preregistration.

- Four previously selected middle sessions (`fip_13`–`fip_16`).
- DMS acquired-sample fluorescence change: mean 0–0.5 s after feedback minus
  mean -0.5–0 s before feedback.
- Within-animal contrast: correct minus incorrect feedback.
- Animal is the aggregation unit; events are not independent population units.
- Descriptive paired-t interval and p-value, with no causal interpretation.
- Required assumptions are recorded in an executable schema-v1 analysis plan.

Feedback was not randomized, the sample contains four animals, and correctness
is entangled with behavior and trial history. The result is an integration
demonstration, not evidence that feedback causes a DMS response difference.
