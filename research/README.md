# Research write-ups

Internal narrative records of individual studies. **This is not user
documentation** and it is not published to the documentation site.

Each file reports one study: what was frozen beforehand, what was run, and what
came out — including negative, mixed, and failed results, which are retained
rather than removed. Version suffixes in the filenames are deliberate: a `v0.1`
and a `v0.2` write-up of the same study are distinct frozen records, and the
later one does not replace the earlier one.

The corresponding frozen protocols and machine-readable outcomes are in
[`../benchmarks/`](../benchmarks/). User-facing method documentation is in
[`../docs/`](../docs/).

`drafts/` holds explicitly non-normative design notes that were superseded. They
are kept for provenance and describe intentions, not behaviour.
